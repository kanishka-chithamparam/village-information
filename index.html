About Thiruvizha

Thiruvizha is a tranquil village known for its agriculture, handloom weaving and yearly temple festival.

History

Founded centuries ago, the village has a strong cultural heritage and community-driven governance.

Economy

Most families rely on farming and weaving, with small businesses in the local market.

Transport

Regular buses connect to Salem; local roads are well maintained with easy access to the highway.

Services & Resources

Health Center

Open weekdays, vaccination drives and maternal health services available.

Education Support

After-school tuition programs and digital learning center for students.

Agriculture Help

Soil testing, subsidised seeds and seasonal workshops for farmers.
